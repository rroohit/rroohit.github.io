# Delete your Frankie account


This page explains how to delete your account in **Frankie**, the everyday-tools app by the
**Sayro team**, and what happens to your data when you do.

You only have an account if you chose to sign in. Frankie works without one.

## Delete your account in the app (fastest)

1. Open Frankie and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap your name at the top to open your profile.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
4. Choose your Google account again to confirm it's you.

Your account is deleted right away.

## Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email **sayro.team@gmail.com** from the
email address on your Frankie account with the subject **"Delete my Frankie account"**. We'll delete
your account and data within **30 days** and reply to confirm.

## What gets deleted

- Your account (name, email address, unique account ID, and account creation date)
- Your profile photo and display name
- Everything backed up to your account: History, Favorites, Saved cities, usage counts, and
  Preferences
- When you delete from the app, all Frankie data on that device too

## What's kept

- **Nothing linked to your account.** Anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) aren't tied to
  your account and are deleted automatically by Google, typically within 90 days.
- Frankie data on *other* devices you used stays on those devices until you uninstall the app or
  clear it there. It is no longer backed up once your account is deleted.

Questions? See the [privacy policy](https://rroohit.github.io/frankie/privacy) or email
**sayro.team@gmail.com**.
