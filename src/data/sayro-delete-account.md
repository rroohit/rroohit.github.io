# Delete your Sayro account

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data. It
applies to the Sayro mobile app.

## How to delete your account

The fastest way is in the app, and it takes effect immediately:

1. Open Sayro.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm.

You do not need to contact us, and there is no waiting period.

## If you cannot access the app

Email **support@sayro.app** from the email address on your account, or including
the phone number you sign in with. We will verify that you control the account
before deleting anything, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your profile and your username reservation
- Every creation and its source photo
- Everything you shared
- Every style you published

## What is kept, and why

- **Images other people generated using your styles.** Those belong to them, not
  to you, so they remain.
- **Internal usage records** used to calculate other creators' activity counts.
  Your account identifier is removed from these, so they no longer identify you.
- **Purchase records.** We are required to keep records of sales as tax and
  accounting records. This is the transaction only, not your photos or your
  creations.
