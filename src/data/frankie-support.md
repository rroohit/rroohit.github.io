# Frankie support


Frankie is an everyday-tools app by the **Sayro team**, for Android and iPhone.

**Need help?** Email **sayro.team@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.
Tell us your device, the app version (Profile → Version) and what happened.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?
No. Every tool works without an account, and offline. Signing in is optional and only adds backup
and sync of your history, favorites, saved cities and settings.

### How do I sync between my Android phone and iPhone?
Sign in with the **same Google account** on both. Sign in with Apple is available on iPhone only,
so an account created with Sign in with Apple can't be opened on Android.

### I used "Hide My Email" with Sign in with Apple. Is that a problem?
No. Apple gives Frankie a private relay address that forwards to you. Just keep using Sign in with
Apple on your iPhone. It does mean that account is separate from any Google account you use.

### It says my email already has a Frankie account with another sign-in method
You first signed up with the other option (Google or Sign in with Apple). Sign in that way instead
and your data will be there.

### The timer didn't alert me
Frankie asks for notification permission the first time you start a timer. If you said no, turn it
on in your phone's Settings → Notifications → Frankie. The timer still counts down inside the app
either way.

### How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: **Profile** → tap your name → **Delete account**. It's deleted right away. Without the
app, see [Delete your Frankie account](https://rroohit.github.io/frankie/delete-account).

### What data does Frankie collect?
See the [privacy policy](https://rroohit.github.io/frankie/privacy). In short: no ads, no tracking.
Without an account, everything stays on your device.
